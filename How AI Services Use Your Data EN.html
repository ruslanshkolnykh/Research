How AI services use your data

A comparison of the consumer privacy policies and terms of Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT), Google (Gemini), DeepSeek, Qwen, Yandex (Alice AI) and Sber (GigaChat). Every statement below comes from the current version of the company's official document, linked in the table and on each company card. No third-party reviews or summaries were used.

Where and how your data is used Where the company states your data is NOT used Not stated in the document

The comparison covers consumer services (chatbots and apps). Business plans and APIs are governed by separate agreements and documents; the bottom row of the table covers the API. Russian-language documents are paraphrased closely in English.

For Yandex, the Alice AI terms and Yandex's general Privacy Policy (26.08.2025) were analysed. For Sber, the GigaChat user agreement and the privacy policy of the developers.sber.ru portal (10.02.2025), through which the GigaChat API is accessed. Sberbank's Personal Data Processing Policy (approved 10.09.2025), referenced by the GigaChat agreement, was analysed from a copy of the page supplied by the user.

Company by company

Anthropic — Claude

Privacy Policy, effective 10 September 2026
Privacy PolicyConsumer TermsHow models are trainedHow to turn off training
  • What is collected: name, email, phone, payment details, your inputs and outputs, feedback, data from connected services, device technical data and IP. For age or identity verification, an ID image and facial geometry templates.
  • Training (section 2): inputs and outputs may be used to train models unless you opt out in your account settings.
  • Exceptions to the opt-out: even after opting out, chats flagged for safety review and material you report through feedback are used. Rating a response with 👍/👎 may store the whole related conversation.
  • De-identification (section 6): feedback and flagged content are disassociated from your user ID before training, but flagged content may be re-identified to enforce policies.
  • Disclosure: to affiliates, vendors, your employer (if the account uses a work email), and authorities under law.
  • "We don't use" statements: Anthropic does not sell personal data; makes no decisions based solely on automated processing or profiling with legal effects; does not collect data from children under 18; the policy does not apply to enterprise customer data.

OpenAI — ChatGPT

Privacy Policy, updated 6 February 2026
Policy (rest of world)EEA, UK, Switzerland versionHow to opt out of trainingprivacy.openai.com
  • What is collected: account data, date of birth, payments, all uploaded content (text, files, images, audio, video, connected-service data), address book contacts if you connect them, logs, device data, approximate and (optionally) precise GPS location, Atlas browser data.
  • Training (section 2): content you provide may be used to train the models behind ChatGPT; you can opt out in settings.
  • Monitoring: content submitted on the platform is monitored to prevent fraud and abuse; a user's age may be estimated.
  • Retention (section 4): deleted data is erased within 30 days, except data already de-identified and disassociated from your account for model training.
  • Disclosure: vendors, affiliates, business account admins (who can see your content), parents of teens with linked accounts, search and shopping partners, authorities.
  • "We don't use" statements: Temporary Chats are not used to improve models; OpenAI does not "sell" personal data, "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising, or process it for targeted advertising; does not process sensitive data to infer consumer characteristics; the policy does not cover API and business product content.

Google — Gemini

Gemini Apps Privacy Notice 29 June 2026, Privacy Hub 24 September 2026
Gemini Apps Privacy HubGoogle Privacy PolicyKeep Activity setting
  • What is collected: prompts, files, photos, screens, Gemini Live recordings and transcripts, Gem instructions, connected app data (Gmail, Photos, Search and YouTube history), call and message logs, contacts, installed apps and screen content in the mobile app, location, remote browser cookies.
  • Use: to provide, improve and develop services, including generative AI models, to personalise and to measure performance.
  • Humans read chats: a subset of chats is reviewed by trained reviewers, including service-provider staff; Google explicitly asks you not to enter confidential information. Reviewed chats are kept up to 3 years and are not deleted when you delete your activity.
  • Retention: activity is auto-deleted after 18 months by default. With Keep Activity off, chats are still kept for 72 hours and used to protect Google and users, including with human reviewers. Gemini settings do not control processing of chats to create anonymised data.
  • "We don't use" statements: with Keep Activity off, future chats are not used to train models (unless you send feedback); temporary chats are not used for training; Gemini chats are not used to show ads; Google does not sell your personal information; Gemini Live audio, video and screen shares are not used to improve services by default; agentic call recordings and transcripts are not used for training; avatar recordings are not used for training except to address abuse; source files in Notebooks are not used directly for training.

DeepSeek

Privacy Policy, updated 10 February 2026
Privacy PolicyModel mechanism and trainingTerms of Use
  • What is collected: email or phone, password, date of birth, text and voice input, files, photos, chat history, IP, device identifiers; a device ID and user ID are assigned to track activity across devices; public internet data for training.
  • Training: data is used to improve and train its technology, including machine learning models, including by monitoring interactions across all your devices.
  • Group companies: process your data for research, foundation model training and optimisation, analytics and storage.
  • Storage: data is collected, processed and stored in the People's Republic of China; kept as long as the account exists.
  • Disclosure: to law enforcement, public authorities and copyright holders under law, legal process or government requests; search keywords are shared with search API providers.
  • Opt-out: the list of rights includes opting out of the use of data for model training; exercised by emailing privacy@deepseek.com.
  • "We don't use" statements: does not extract voiceprints, facial recognition data or other biometrics from voice and photos; does not obtain precise geolocation without explicit consent; does not engage in targeted advertising, sell personal data or use it for profiling; the policy does not cover third-party apps built on the DeepSeek API.

Qwen

Privacy Policy, effective 7 September 2026
Privacy PolicySame policy via chat.qwen.ai
  • What is collected: prompts, text, files, images, audio, video, voice and video recordings when using voice features, account data (including from Google, Apple, GitHub), feedback (👍/👎 stores the conversation), device data and IP, logs; data about you from advertising and analytics partners.
  • Training (sections II and IV): de-identified user content and feedback may be used to train and improve models on the basis of "legitimate interests". No separate opt-out is described.
  • Disclosure: Alibaba Cloud (Singapore) processes requests and stores data; some automatically collected device data is shared with advertising and analytics partners; affiliates receive data for research, analytics and content moderation.
  • Storage: processed and stored in Singapore; kept "as long as necessary".
  • "We don't use" statements: raw inputs are not used to train models directly; inputs are not reviewed manually except for security, abuse, debugging and legal compliance; Alibaba Cloud deletes your input after generating a response and does not store it separately; no decisions based on automated processing with legal effects.

Yandex — Alice AI

Alice AI Terms of Use of 10 September 2026; app licence agreement of 10 April 2026
Alice AI Terms of UseAlice AI app licenceYandex Privacy PolicyOpt out of service improvement
  • Operator: Platforma II-resheniy LLC (Moscow). The service runs in the Alice AI and "Yandex with Alice" apps, Yandex Browser, Search and at alice.yandex.ru. Russian law applies.
  • What is collected (app licence, cl. 5.8): OS type, SIM card details, location, app identifier, usage statistics, linked device data, voice recordings, search queries. When Alice is called in Yandex Browser, the content of the open page, including personal data, may be processed (cl. 2.8). The booking agent uses name, address, phone and email from Yandex ID and your booking history (cl. 2.12.2).
  • Service improvement (cl. 6.6): Yandex may use requests, their parts, uploaded materials and any data obtained by executing requests to improve the services of Yandex and its affiliates. You can opt out in Yandex ID settings.
  • Licence to requests (cl. 4.3): by submitting a request you grant Yandex a free, irrevocable, worldwide licence to the request and uploaded files, including video and camera images: to improve algorithms and technologies (anonymously), to place them in Yandex services for public access, anonymously or linked to your name, with the right to sublicense to third parties without your consent.
  • Service responses (cl. 3.4): any generated content may be used by Yandex and its affiliates anonymously, for any purpose, worldwide, with no time limit.
  • Statistics (cl. 6.5): data is processed by Yandex and its affiliates for research and statistics, including to provide to third parties; published only in aggregate.
  • Voice recordings (app licence): de-identified recordings may be accessed by Yandex staff and contractors to analyse and improve the app. Voice data settings are available in your account.
  • Storage (cl. 6.2): consent to storage and processing in other jurisdictions, including Russia, lasts for the period of use and 10 years after. Data is destroyed once the purposes are achieved.
  • General Privacy Policy (26.08.2025): applies to Yandex LLC and group companies. It collects search history, purchases, places visited, likes, email correspondents' addresses, phone book data, files stored with Yandex, location, Wi-Fi and payment card data; data from different services is linked to your account (s. 4). Yandex also receives data from partners such as advertisers and taxi services.
  • Purposes (s. 5): personalising ads and offers by search history and other data, improving other Yandex products, creating new products, big data and research, checking trustworthiness.
  • Disclosure (s. 7): to Yandex group companies; ad networks, advertisers, site and app owners; taxi services; payment systems; authorities, law enforcement and courts on mandatory request. Russian citizens' data is stored in databases in Russia (s. 7.3).
  • Controls: delete data in Yandex ID "Data management"; turn off ad personalisation at yandex.ru/tune/adv.
  • "We don't use" statements: Yandex does not intentionally collect sensitive data (race, political views, health, biometrics), though it processes such data if you enter it yourself, for example in a health query; does not collect data to build a user "profile" to an extent that significantly affects rights and freedoms; data is mostly processed automatically without staff access. For voice dictation of emails and calendar entries, recordings are not transferred or used; statistical results are published only in a form that does not identify users. Yandex also states it has no technical means or obligation to moderate or view posted information (cl. 5.4).

Sber — GigaChat

GigaChat Service User Agreement, updated 22 June 2026
GigaChat User AgreementGigaChat API offer for individualsSberbank Personal Data Policy
  • Operator: Sberbank PJSC. The service is available via the Bank's portal and API; users must be adult, legally capable Russian residents. Personal data is processed under Sberbank's Personal Data Policy (cl. 7.1).
  • Training: the agreement says neither that requests are used for training nor that they are not. Users may not use what they obtain from the service to develop competing models (cl. 4.5.7).
  • Licence to your requests (cl. 5.3): by posting content in the service or sending it via the API, you grant the Bank a non-exclusive, free licence for the full term of the right, worldwide and by any means; for databases, including extraction of materials.
  • Licence to outputs (cl. 5.2): an irrevocable, free licence to generated content: storage on the servers of the Bank and its affiliates, sublicensing to third parties, public display for promotion and advertising, modification, use in the Bank's advertising and marketing materials.
  • Personal data in requests (cl. 7.1–7.6): the service is not intended for requests containing personal data; users undertake not to send it, and if they do, they are responsible for the lawfulness of processing and must compensate the Bank's losses.
  • Disclosure: partners Zvuk LLC and RIKh LLC receive a digital identifier, generated and user content and technical details when you launch their service (cl. 7.9).
  • Storage: on servers designated by the Bank; location and term not stated.
  • developers.sber.ru portal privacy policy (10.02.2025): operator Sberbank PJSC; the GigaChat API is accessed through this portal. Collects identification data (including via Sber ID), account data, data from third parties, including Sber group companies, and cookies (s. 2). Purposes: running and improving the portal, promotional mailings (cl. 3.1). Disclosure: to other portal participants, third parties under contracts or with your consent, and authorities under law, including inquiry and investigation bodies (s. 6). Term: destroyed within 30 days after the purpose is achieved (cl. 7.1). Data requests to pdn-org@sberbank.ru, answered within 30 days (cl. 9.1). The policy says nothing about AI training, sale of data or storage location.
  • Sberbank Personal Data Processing Policy (approved by Management Board resolution No. 18§12a of 10.09.2025; sberbank.ru/privacy/policy). Referenced by the GigaChat agreement (cl. 7.1). This is the bank's general document: AI, GigaChat and chatbot requests are not mentioned separately. Of its 16 processing purposes, these matter for customers:
    • Purpose 5, personalised offers: identifying interests, including those detected by the Bank's automated systems; building a single customer profile; assessing contract terms for a personal offer; also covers partners' customers and website and app users.
    • Purpose 6, information and advertising: advertising by calls, SMS, push and email, including about partners' and third parties' projects.
    • Purpose 7, product development: analytics, improving existing products and developing new ones using data on website and app activity, transactions, income, spending, interests, family and children, social media profile, receipts and enquiries.
  • Disclosure (cl. 9.2): the Bank may transfer data to third parties and partners, authorities, extra-budgetary funds, the Bank of Russia and courts, and engage them to process it where there is a legal basis. A separate purpose 16 covers dealings with foreign authorities.
  • Storage and retention: Russian citizens' data is stored in databases in Russia, except as provided by law (cl. 9.1); kept no longer than the purposes require (cl. 4.3). Destroyed when the purpose is achieved, consent is withdrawn or processing must stop (cl. 4.4); specific terms are set in the Bank's internal documents.
  • Rights (cl. 9.3): obtain information about processing, demand correction, blocking or destruction of data, stop processing, withdraw consent. The Bank may still process data without consent where the law allows (cl. 9.2).
  • "We don't use" statements (general policy): not combining databases processed for incompatible purposes; processing only data relevant to the purposes; keeping data no longer than the purposes require (principles, cl. 2.1). There is no statement that data is not sold.
  • "We don't use" statements (GigaChat): the user agreement contains none. The separate offer for the paid GigaChat API for individuals (SaluteDevices LLC, 06.02.2026) states that customer content is not used for own purposes unrelated to providing the service and that generated content may be used in marketing only with the customer's prior consent (cl. 6.2–6.3).
